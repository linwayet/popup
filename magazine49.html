區塊一　英文文章原文

Telehealth：遠距醫療如何把病人與醫療資源連起來

Discussions of technical progress and its effect on the medical field often focus on new treatments for diseases. But technology can also play a role in solving a different problem: accessibility. Sometimes people have diseases that can be cured, but they live too far from a hospital to receive treatment. Or the hospitals they are able to reach may not have a doctor who specializes in that disease. If they could receive medical care from a distance, it might save their lives. And telehealth offers that very possibility.

Telehealth refers to medical care provided when the doctor and patient are not physically in the same place, including video conferences with doctors that replace office visits. Telehealth also allows patients to send text messages to doctors or nurses between visits. It can even involve patients wearing sensors or monitors at home and this data being sent to their doctor.

Telehealth has some distinct advantages over conventional medicine. First, it avoids the risk of patients catching contagious diseases from other patients. Also, whoever needs medical care can meet with a doctor without making the trip to a hospital, which is especially important for people living in remote or rural areas. Remote monitoring of patients also allows doctors to gain information over an extended time without keeping the patient hospitalized.

Currently, telehealth is mostly used for verbal consultations and remote monitoring of patients. But since 2001, technology has made remote surgery possible, which is now happening more and more often.

Last year, researchers in Dundee, Scotland, removed a blood clot from a dead human body using a robot that they controlled from a hospital across the city. A few hours later, a surgeon based in the United States did the same procedure. Even across the Atlantic Ocean, the time between the surgeon moving his instruments and the robot doing the same was only a fraction of a second. The doctor in Dundee commented that the remote surgery felt just like performing a normal operation.

The operation the robots were used for is used to treat stroke patients. It requires special training, and a delay of even a few minutes can be deadly. A patient who has a stroke in a small town could die before reaching a hospital with a doctor qualified to perform the surgery. But hospitals in small towns could adopt remote surgery technology once it is developed and becomes affordable, allowing surgeons in larger hospitals to perform life-saving surgery on patients who otherwise couldn’t get it.

Telehealth is already making medical care more accessible and convenient, and technical advances could open up amazing possibilities, such as remote surgery.

How effective is telehealth?

Many studies have shown telehealth to be as effective as in-person care for the types of care it can provide. In fact, in some areas such as mental health, telehealth may even be more effective.

有時候，人們罹患的是可以治癒的疾病，卻因為住得離醫院太遠而無法接受治療。

③

If they could receive medical care from a distance, it might save their lives.

如果他們能夠遠距接受醫療照護，這可能就能挽救他們的生命。

文法解析：If + 過去式 could 搭配主句 might，呈現較假設性的條件。這種寫法不是單純敘述現在事實，而是想像「若能做到」所可能產生的結果。

④

Telehealth refers to medical care provided when the doctor and patient are not physically in the same place, including video conferences with doctors that replace office visits.

遠距醫療是指醫師與病人並未實際處在同一地點時所提供的醫療照護，其中包括以視訊會診取代到診間看診。

文法解析：provided 是過去分詞，修飾 medical care，可還原為 medical care that is provided。including ... 為補充說明；that replace office visits 是形容詞子句，修飾 video conferences。

Also, whoever needs medical care can meet with a doctor without making the trip to a hospital, which is especially important for people living in remote or rural areas.

文法解析：whoever needs medical care 是複合關係代名詞 whoever 引導的名詞子句，整個子句在句中當主詞。without making ... 使用介係詞 without + V-ing。which 指前面整件事，形成非限定形容詞子句。

Remote monitoring of patients also allows doctors to gain information over an extended time without keeping the patient hospitalized.

遠端監測病患也能讓醫師在較長的一段時間內持續取得資訊，而不必讓病人一直住院。

文法解析：allow + O + to V 表示「允許／使某人能做某事」。without keeping the patient hospitalized 中是 keep + O + OC，hospitalized 為過去分詞形容詞，說明病人的狀態。

⑦

Even across the Atlantic Ocean, the time between the surgeon moving his instruments and the robot doing the same was only a fraction of a second.

即使隔著大西洋，從外科醫師移動器械到機器人做出相同動作之間的時間差，也只有不到一秒的一小部分。

文法解析：between A and B 平行結構中，A 與 B 都使用「名詞 + V-ing」：the surgeon moving ... / the robot doing ...。a fraction of 表示「…的一小部分」。

between A and B 在A與B之間a fraction of …的一小部分do the same 做同樣的事
⑧

But hospitals in small towns could adopt remote surgery technology once it is developed and becomes affordable, allowing surgeons in larger hospitals to perform life-saving surgery on patients who otherwise couldn’t get it.

不過，一旦遠距手術技術發展成熟且價格變得可負擔，小城鎮的醫院就可能採用它，讓大醫院的外科醫師為原本無法接受這種治療的病人進行救命手術。

文法解析：once 引導時間副詞子句；allowing ... 是現在分詞片語，補充前面採用技術所帶來的結果。allow + O + to V 再次出現；who otherwise couldn’t get it 是形容詞子句，修飾 patients。

區塊四　文法解析

文法一　If + S + could..., S + might...

【引句 Quote】

If they could receive medical care from a distance, it might save their lives.

【句型】

If + S + could + V, S + might + V.

【核心說明】

這是帶有假設意味的條件句。could 表示「若有能力／若能做到」，might 則保留結果的不確定性。文章用它來想像遠距醫療若能解決距離問題，可能帶來的生命救援效果。

【使用規則】
  • if 子句用 could + 原形動詞，可表較不確定或假設性的條件。
  • 主句可用 would / could / might + 原形動詞，依語意表結果、能力或可能性。
  • 不要在 if 子句中直接用 would 表一般假設條件。
  • 若只是陳述很可能發生的未來條件，通常可用 If + 現在式, will + V。
【主題例句】

If remote surgery became widely available, more rural patients might receive treatment in time.
如果遠距手術變得普及，更多偏鄉病人可能可以及時接受治療。

【學習提醒】

看到 could / might 不要只翻成「可以／可能」。先判斷它們是否共同建立一個「假設情境」。這會影響整句的語氣強度。

【⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題】

✗ If patients would receive care remotely, it might save their lives.

✓ If patients could receive care remotely, it might save their lives.

一般假設條件的 if 子句不用 would；此處用 could 表「若能夠」。

Choose the best answer: If the system became cheaper, more hospitals ___ adopt it.　(A) might　(B) must to　(C) will be　(D) had

文法二　whoever 引導名詞子句

【引句 Quote】

Whoever needs medical care can meet with a doctor without making the trip to a hospital.

【句型】

Whoever + V ... + singular V ...

【核心說明】

whoever 可理解為 anyone who，意思是「任何…的人」。它本身同時包含先行詞與關係代名詞的功能，因此 whoever needs medical care 整體可以直接當句子的主詞。

【使用規則】
  • whoever = anyone who，指人。
  • 整個 whoever 子句可作主詞、受詞或補語。
  • whoever 後面必須形成完整的關係子句結構。
  • 不要在 whoever 前再加 the person，否則會重複。
【主題例句】

Whoever lives far from a major hospital may benefit from telehealth.
任何住得離大型醫院很遠的人，都可能從遠距醫療受益。

【學習提醒】

whoever 與 who 不同。who 常是疑問詞或關係代名詞；whoever 帶有「無論誰／任何…的人」的泛指語意。

【⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題】

✗ The person whoever needs help can call this number.

✓ Whoever needs help can call this number.

whoever 已包含 anyone/the person 的概念，前面不再加先行詞。

Choose the best answer: ___ needs specialist advice can request an online consultation.　(A) Whoever　(B) Which　(C) Where　(D) Whose

文法三　allow + O + to V 與分詞結果

【引句 Quote】

Hospitals could adopt remote surgery technology ..., allowing surgeons in larger hospitals to perform life-saving surgery.

【句型】

S + V ..., allowing + O + to + V.

【核心說明】

allow + O + to V 表示「讓／允許某人做某事」。文章再把 allowing 放在逗號後，形成現在分詞片語，用來補充前面整件事帶來的結果。

【使用規則】
  • allow 後面先接受詞，再接 to + 原形動詞。
  • to 是不定詞標記，不是介係詞。
  • allowing 位於逗號後時，常補充前句造成的自然結果。
  • 若要表正式被允許，可用 be allowed to + V。
【主題例句】

Better networks could reduce delays, allowing specialists to guide treatment from another city.
更好的網路可以降低延遲，進而讓專科醫師從另一座城市指導治療。

【學習提醒】

常見錯誤是寫成 allow someone do。英文標準用法是 allow someone to do；若換成 let，才是 let someone do。

【⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題】

✗ The system allows doctors monitor patients at home.

✓ The system allows doctors to monitor patients at home.

allow + O 後面必須接 to V。

Correct the sentence: Telehealth allows rural patients receiving specialist advice without long trips.
