去年夏天，我和家人到一座小島旅行。我們先搭乘渡輪，船票不算貴。途中，我們看到一架直升機從頭頂飛過，遠處的天空還有一架噴射機。抵達後，一輛老舊的吉普車載我們到海邊的一間木屋。

Last summer, my family went on an exciting journey to a small island. We first took a ferry, and the fare was quite cheap. On the way, we saw a helicopter flying above us and a jet far away in the sky. When we arrived, an old jeep took us to a wooden hut near the sea.

那間木屋雖然簡單，卻很舒適。裡面有小壁爐、暖氣、微波爐，甚至還有冷凍庫。屋主用木製桌椅布置房間。屋外有一道白色籬笆，圍著一座小花園，裡面種著萵苣、蘑菇和甜瓜。

The hut was simple but comfortable. It had a small fireplace, a heater, a microwave, and even a freezer. The owner had used wooden tables and chairs to furnish the rooms. Outside, a white fence surrounded a small garden full of lettuce, mushrooms, and melon.

我們到附近的雜貨店買食物，買了一條麵包、一些麵粉、水餃、甜甜圈、果凍和一瓶檸檬水。我們還找到一些多汁的水果，以及一支色彩鮮豔的棒棒糖給妹妹。還沒走出店門，她就開心地開始舔棒棒糖。爸爸本來想買些酒，但媽媽提醒他，我們接下來還有很長的一天。

We went to a nearby grocery store to buy food. We bought a loaf of bread, some flour, dumplings, a doughnut, jelly, and a bottle of lemonade. We also found some juicy fruit and a colorful lollipop for my little sister. She began to lick it happily before we even left the store. My father wanted to buy some liquor, but my mother reminded him that we still had a long day ahead of us.

那天晚上，我們一起煮晚餐。水餃的味道很棒，新鮮的甜瓜也特別香甜。晚餐後，我們一起做家事。妹妹幫忙洗衣服，我則整理廚房。我學到，當大家住在一個小地方時，分擔家庭事務是非常重要的。

That evening, we cooked dinner together. The dumplings had a wonderful flavor, and the fresh melon tasted especially sweet. After dinner, we did some housework. My sister helped with the laundry, while I cleaned the kitchen. I learned that sharing household duties is essential when everyone is staying in a small place.

隔天早上，我們參觀一座當地文化中心。館內有一台老式電梯。導覽員向我們展示，人們以前如何使用天然染料替亞麻布、皮革和羊毛染色。一位女士還教我們如何編織圍巾。我們也看到傳統服飾，包括一件漂亮的長袍和一條手工領帶。

The next morning, we visited a local cultural center. There was an old elevator inside the building. The guide showed us how people used natural dye to color linen, leather, and wool. One woman taught us how to knit a scarf. We also saw traditional clothes, including a beautiful gown and a handmade necktie.

後來，我們參觀了一座以移民為主題的博物館。館內講述許多人渡海展開新生活的故事。有些人只帶著少量食物、簡單衣物和基本裝備。他們的經歷讓我明白，這樣的旅程可能有多麼艱難。

Later, we visited a museum about immigration. It told the stories of people who had crossed the sea to begin new lives. Some had carried only a little food, simple clothing, and basic gear. Their experiences made me realize how difficult such a journey could be.

下午，當地的節慶開始了。人們在街上列隊行進，樂手演奏著歡快的歌曲。走了好幾個小時後，我們回到木屋，小睡了一會兒。

In the afternoon, a local festival began. People started to march through the streets while musicians played cheerful songs. After walking for hours, we returned to the hut and took a short nap.

這並不是一趟豪華的假期，卻教會我一件重要的事：最棒的旅行不一定需要昂貴的飯店或知名景點。有時候，簡單的食物、新鮮的體驗，以及和家人共度的時光，就足以讓一趟旅程變得難以忘懷。

It was not a luxurious vacation, but it taught me something important: the best trips are not always about expensive hotels or famous places. Sometimes, simple food, new experiences, and time with family are enough to make a journey unforgettable.
