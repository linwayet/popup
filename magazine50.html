上個週末，我和家人到一座海邊小鎮旅行。我們登上一架由當地航空公司營運的小型飛機。起飛前，因為機場附近有一輛救護車正在協助一名身體不適的乘客，我們等了將近一個小時。最後，飛機終於準備出發，我們的假期也正式開始。

Last weekend, my family and I went on a short trip to a seaside town. We went aboard a small plane operated by a local airline. Before the plane’s departure, we had to wait for almost an hour because an ambulance was helping a sick passenger near the airport. Finally, the plane was ready to depart, and our vacation began.

抵達後，我們租了一輛汽車，開往海岸附近的一間小屋。這間小屋從外觀看起來很簡單，但裡面溫暖又舒適。屋內有石造煙囪、木製書櫃、大型櫥櫃，以及放滿盤子和杯子的碗櫥。我們的臥室裡還有一個小衣櫥，可以掛衣服。

After landing, we rented an automobile and drove to a small cottage near the coast. The cottage looked simple from the outside, but it was warm and comfortable inside. There was a stone chimney, a wooden bookcase, a large cabinet, and a cupboard filled with plates and cups. Our bedroom also had a small closet where we could hang our clothes.

隔天早上，我們到附近一家餐廳吃早午餐。那裡提供種類豐富的自助餐。我選了培根、酥脆麵包、一些乳製品和幾顆杏仁。妹妹喝的是用新鮮椰子做成的椰奶，而爸媽則點了咖啡。那裡也有好幾種甜點，不過其中有些對我來說味道太苦。

The next morning, we went to a nearby restaurant for brunch. It offered a huge buffet with many kinds of food. I chose some bacon, crispy bread, dairy products, and a few almonds. My sister drank coconut milk made from fresh coconut, while my parents ordered coffee. There were also several kinds of dessert, but some of them tasted too bitter for me.

媽媽提醒我們要慢慢咀嚼食物，不要吃得太快。當天晚上，我們決定到一家海鮮餐廳用餐。爸爸點了一杯含有少量酒精的雞尾酒，而我們其他人則喝果汁。

My mother reminded us to chew our food slowly instead of eating too quickly. Later that evening, we decided to dine at a seafood restaurant. My father ordered a cocktail containing a small amount of alcohol, while the rest of us had fruit juice.

晚餐後，我們到夜市散步。妹妹看到一件有白色衣領的漂亮女襯衫，但她覺得價格太高。和老闆稍微議價之後，她終於用比較便宜的價格買下來，並用自己每週零用錢的一部分付款。

After dinner, we walked through a night market. My sister found a beautiful blouse with a white collar, but she thought it was too expensive. After a little bargain with the shop owner, she finally bought it at a lower price. She used part of her weekly allowance to pay for it.

第三天，我們參觀一座老鐵路博物館。館內有一節木製客車車廂，我們也學到早期火車的運作方式。導覽員向我們展示老式煞車系統，並說明它如何幫助火車安全停下來。附近還有一台大型起重機，正在搬運沉重的設備。

The following day, we visited an old railway museum. Inside, we saw a wooden passenger carriage and learned how early trains worked. The guide showed us an old-fashioned brake system and explained how it helped trains stop safely. Nearby, a huge crane was being used to move some heavy equipment.

離開小鎮之前，我們還參觀了一座小型文化中心。幾位當地人穿著傳統服裝，還邀請我們一起拍照。這趟旅行雖然只有三天，卻讓我們有機會享受美食、認識地方歷史，也能和家人共度美好時光。

Before leaving the town, we also visited a small cultural center. Some local people were wearing traditional costumes, and we were invited to take pictures with them. The trip lasted only three days, but it gave us a wonderful chance to enjoy good food, learn about local history, and spend time together as a family.
