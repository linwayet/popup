Reading Skill Builder – Cause and Effect

Understanding cause and effect helps readers see how events are connected.

A cause is why something happens, and an effect is what happens because of a cause.

This is especially helpful when reading nonfiction, in which authors often explain how actions, decisions, or events lead to certain results.

When identifying causes and effects, look out for these signal words.

Words and phrases that signal a cause, because, since, due to, on account of, as a result of, result from.

Words and phrases that signal an effect, therefore, thus, consequently, accordingly, hence, as As a result, lead to result in.

區塊四　文法解析

① Although 引導讓步副詞子句

【引句 Quote】

Although cow milk is widely consumed in western countries, goat milk is actually more popular worldwide, with roughly 65 to 72% of the world's population consuming it.

【句型】

Although + S + V, S + V ...

【核心說明】

Although 表示「雖然、儘管」，用來提出與主句形成對比的背景資訊。重點通常落在主句，而不是 although 子句。

【使用規則】

  • Although 後面必須接完整子句。
  • Although 與 but 不可在同一組句子中重複使用。
  • Although 可放句首，也可放在主句之後。
  • 若句子很正式，也可用 though / even though 表達相似概念。

【主題例句】

Although goat milk contains lactose, some people find it easier to digest.
雖然山羊奶含有乳糖，有些人仍覺得它比較容易消化。

【學習提醒】

中文常說「雖然……但是……」，但英文若用了 although，後面的主句通常不再加 but。

【⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題】

✗ Although cow milk is common, but goat milk is popular worldwide.

✓ Although cow milk is common, goat milk is popular worldwide.

選出正確句：A. Although it is expensive, but many people buy it.　B. Although it is expensive, many people buy it.

② where 引導形容詞子句

【引句 Quote】

One reason is that goats are easier to raise in diverse climates, including dry or mountainous regions where cows may struggle.

【句型】

place / region + where + S + V

【核心說明】

where 是關係副詞，用來修飾表示地點的先行詞。regions where cows may struggle 就是「乳牛可能難以適應的地區」。

【使用規則】

  • 先行詞通常是 place、region、country、area 等地點名詞。
  • where 在子句中相當於 in / at which。
  • where 後面接完整的主詞與動詞。
  • 不要把 where 與 which 在同一位置重複使用。

【主題例句】

Goats can live in areas where the climate is dry and mountainous.
山羊可以生活在氣候乾燥又多山的地區。

【學習提醒】

若關係詞本身要當動詞的受詞，通常使用 which / that；若表示「在那個地方」，才用 where。

【⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題】

✗ This is a region which goats can live easily.

✓ This is a region where goats can live easily.

填入適當關係詞：This is the farm ______ the goats are raised.

③ Cause and Effect：because / so / therefore / result in

【引句 Quote】

A cause is why something happens, and an effect is what happens because of a cause.

【句型】

Cause + because / since ... ; Effect + so / therefore ...

【核心說明】

因果關係是非小說文章中非常常見的組織方式。because、since、due to 等通常提示原因；so、therefore、consequently、result in 等通常提示結果。

【使用規則】

  • because + 完整子句；because of / due to + 名詞或 V-ing。
  • so 可連接兩個子句，表示前因導致後果。
  • therefore / consequently 常用作句子副詞，前後需注意標點。
  • result in = 導致；result from = 起因於，方向相反。

【主題例句】

Goat milk has smaller fat particles; therefore, some people find it easier to digest.
山羊奶的脂肪微粒較小；因此，有些人覺得它比較容易消化。

【學習提醒】

最常見錯誤是把 result in 與 result from 混淆。A results in B 表示 A 導致 B；B results from A 表示 B 起因於 A。

【⚠️ 常見錯誤 / 🎯 精選試題】

✗ Smaller fat particles result from easier digestion.

✓ Smaller fat particles may result in easier digestion for some people.

選出正確搭配：A. result in a change　B. result from a change（若要表達「導致改變」，答案為 A）

練文筆食材 ⌄

① Although + S + V, S + V

適用：用於先承認一個事實，再提出對比重點

Although cow milk is common, goat milk is more popular in many parts of the world.

② One reason is that + S + V

適用：用於提出原因

One reason is that goats can live in diverse climates.

③ be easier to + V + than ...

適用：用於比較兩者難易度

Goat milk may be easier to digest than cow milk for some people.

④ because + S + V

適用：用於直接說明原因

Some people choose goat milk because its fat particles are smaller.

⑤ which can + V

適用：用於補充前述特性的可能結果

The proteins are slightly different, which can affect digestion.

⑥ so + S + V

適用：用於接續直接結果

It contains lactose, so it may not suit everyone.

⑦ Meanwhile, + S + V

適用：用於切換到同時存在的另一情況

Meanwhile, cow milk remains widely available.

⑧ make + O + C

適用：用於表達「使某物成為／呈現某狀態」

Large-scale production makes cow milk a staple in many markets.

⑨ When + V-ing, ...

適用：用於簡化表示時間或情境的副詞子句

When identifying causes, look for signal words.

⑩ lead to + N

適用：用於表達導致某結果

Changes in diet can lead to changes in digestion.

⑪ result in + N

適用：用於表達「導致」

A supply shortage may result in higher prices.

⑫ result from + N

適用：用於表達「起因於」

Digestive discomfort may result from lactose intolerance.

⑬ as a result

適用：用於承接前因並強調後果

Some people cannot digest lactose well. As a result, they choose lactose-free products.

⑭ due to + N / V-ing

適用：用於較正式地表示原因

Goat milk is common in some regions due to the ease of raising goats.

⑮ in which + S + V

適用：用於修飾地點、情境或抽象名詞

Nonfiction is a type of writing in which authors often explain cause and effect.
